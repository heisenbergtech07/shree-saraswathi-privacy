Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shree Saraswathi Educational Group of Institution ("we," "us," "the Center") collects, uses, stores, and protects information through our student management app — used by the Center's admin staff and teachers to manage students, and by students/parents through the student portal (web and Android app).

This app is operated by the Center for its own internal use. Accounts are created only by Center staff for enrolled students and employed teachers/admins — the app does not offer public sign-up.

Information We Collect

Student Information (entered by Center staff during admission)

  • Full name, date of birth, gender, photograph
  • Aadhaar number and a scanned copy of the Aadhaar document, for identity verification during admission
  • Residential address, city, and pincode
  • Class, batch, and admission date
  • Parent/guardian name, relationship, WhatsApp number, and alternate phone number

Academic & Attendance Records

  • Daily attendance (present/absent)
  • Exam names, subjects, and marks obtained
  • Homework assignments and completion status
  • Study materials and links shared with a class

Fee Records

  • Fee structure, amount paid, balance due, and due dates
  • Payment history: amount, date, payment method, and reference number

Login & Account Information

  • Staff: name, email address, and role (admin or teacher)
  • Students: an auto-generated Student ID and password, used only to sign in to the student portal

Communication Records

  • A log of notification messages (e.g. attendance updates, fee reminders) sent to parents, so staff can track what was communicated and when

How We Use This Information

  • To manage student admissions, attendance, academic records, and fee collection
  • To generate printable documents (admission forms, fee receipts, marksheets)
  • To let staff message parents about attendance, marks, and fee status via WhatsApp
  • To let students/parents view attendance, results, fees, homework, and learning material through the student portal
  • To schedule and join live online classes
  • To operate and secure staff and student logins

We do not use student or parent data for advertising, and we do not sell personal information to any third party.

How Information Is Stored & Protected

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider, with the following protections:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Database-level access rules (Row Level Security) restrict who can see what: staff (admin/teacher) accounts can see records relevant to running the Center; a student login can only see that student's own attendance, results, fees, and homework — never another student's
  • Fee and payment data is restricted to admin accounts only; teachers cannot view it
  • Student photos and Aadhaar documents are stored in a private file storage bucket, accessible only to signed-in staff via short-lived, expiring links — never a permanent public URL
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase's secure authentication system

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage
  • Jitsi Meet (meet.jit.si) — provides the live video call functionality for online classes. Video/audio during a class is handled entirely by Jitsi's service and is not recorded or stored by this app
  • WhatsApp — messages to parents are sent manually by staff via a standard WhatsApp link; this app does not use any automated bulk-messaging service, and message content is only what the staff member chooses to send
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces on this app's pages

These providers may process data as necessary to provide their service to us; we do not permit them to use the data for their own independent purposes.

Children's Information

Because this is a tuition/education management app, a large portion of the information it holds relates to students who may be minors. This information is entered and managed exclusively by Center staff as part of the admission and enrollment process, with the knowledge and cooperation of the student's parent/guardian — students do not create their own accounts or submit their own information through an open sign-up process. A student's login is created by staff and shared with the student/parent directly.

Data Retention

Student, attendance, academic, and fee records are retained for as long as the student is enrolled at the Center and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes (e.g. re-admission, certificates, or fee history requests). Staff can deactivate or delete a student's record and associated login at any time through the app.

Your Rights

Parents/guardians and staff may contact the Center to:

  • Request a copy of the information held about a student
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of a student's record and login, subject to the Center's record-keeping obligations

See our Data Deletion Requests page for the exact steps and what's deleted vs. retained.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app's features change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
