Data Deletion Requests

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

This page explains how a student, parent, or staff member can request that their personal data be deleted from the Shree Saraswathi Educational Group of Institution app, and what happens to that data once a request is made. It supplements our full Privacy Policy.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Contact the Center using any of the details below and state that you are requesting deletion of a student's (or your own staff) data.
  2. Include the student's full name and Student ID (or the staff member's name and email) so we can correctly identify the record to delete.
  3. We will verify the request comes from the student's parent/guardian, the student themself (if an adult), or the staff member concerned, before acting on it.
  4. We will delete the applicable data as described below, normally within 30 days of a verified request, and confirm once it's done.

What Gets Deleted, and What We May Keep

Most information is deleted in full. A small amount may be retained for a limited time where we have a genuine administrative, legal, or accounting reason to do so — never for marketing or any other purpose.

Data What happens on request
Name, date of birth, gender, photo, Aadhaar number & document, address, contact details Deleted in full, within 30 days
Login credentials (Student ID / staff login) Deactivated and deleted, within 30 days
Attendance, marks, homework, and learning records Deleted in full, within 30 days
Fee & payment transaction records May be retained for as long as required by applicable accounting/tax law in India (typically several years), since these are financial records, not just personal data. Retained records are restricted to admin access only and are not used for any purpose beyond legal/accounting compliance.

Questions

If you have questions about a deletion request or this process, contact us using the details above, or see our full Privacy Policy.
