By signing in, you agree to them. If you are a student under 18, your parent or guardian agrees to them on your behalf. How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Who Can Use the App

  • The app is only for the Center's enrolled students, their parents or guardians, and the Center's staff. There is no public sign-up.
  • Logins are created by the Center. Keep your Student ID, email, and password private, and don't share your login with anyone outside your family.
  • Tell the Center straight away if you think someone else knows your password, so it can be reset.

Using the App Properly

When you use the app, please don't:

  • Upload anything other than photos of your own homework. No photos of other people, and nothing offensive, copied, or unrelated to the assignment.
  • Try to see another student's information, or get into parts of the app you haven't been given access to.
  • Share online class links, other students' details, or study materials outside the Center without permission.
  • Behave disrespectfully in online classes. The usual classroom rules of the Center apply there too.

The Center may remove uploaded content or suspend a login that breaks these rules.

Information in the App

  • Attendance, marks, fees, homework, and notices are entered by the Center's staff. We work to keep them correct, but if something looks wrong, contact the Center. The Center's own records are final.
  • Fee amounts shown in the app are for information. Payments are made directly to the Center, not through the app.
  • Homework photos are kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically, so keep your own copy if you need one.

Notifications and Messages

With your permission, the app sends phone notifications about homework, marks, notices, online classes, and fee due dates. Staff may also message parents on WhatsApp about attendance, marks, and fees. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

Online Classes

Live online classes run on Jitsi Meet, a third-party video service. Your use of it is also covered by Jitsi's own terms. Classes are not recorded by this app.

  • We try to keep the app running at all times but can't promise it will always be available or free of errors.
  • From time to time a new version will be required to keep using the app. You'll be asked to update from Google Play when that happens.
  • We may change these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes will be announced in the app.

Ending Use of the App

When a student leaves the Center, their login is deactivated. You can ask for your data to be deleted as described on our Data Deletion Requests page.
